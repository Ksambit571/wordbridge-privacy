01 What we collect

WordBridge itself does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or hand over your name, email, or any personal details. There is no WordBridge server collecting or storing information about you.

The only outside data collection happens through the advertising shown in the app, described in full below.

No account, no personal profile. Your score, level and hint count live only in the app on your own device.

02 How advertising works

WordBridge shows ads through Google AdMob, in two places:

  • A small banner beneath the puzzle while you play.
  • An optional "watch an ad to continue" screen after you run out of lives — you choose whether to watch it.

To select, serve, and measure these ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK running inside the app may collect:

Data handled by Google AdMob, not by WordBridge directly
DataWhy
Advertising IDResettable device identifier used to serve and frequency-cap ads.
IP address / approximate locationCoarse region-level targeting, not precise GPS location.
Device informationModel, OS version, language — used for ad formatting and fraud prevention.
Ad interaction dataImpressions, clicks, and rewarded-ad completions.

This collection and its use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and by how Google uses information from apps that use its services. WordBridge does not receive or store this data itself.

03 Data stored on your device

Your high score and best-run stats are saved locally on your device using standard Android app storage. This information:

  • Never leaves your device or gets sent to WordBridge or any server we run.
  • Is removed automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

04 Children's privacy

WordBridge is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

05 Your choices

  • Ad personalization — you can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android versions).
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored WordBridge data from your device.

07 Data security

WordBridge does not operate a backend server and does not transmit your gameplay data anywhere. Any data handled by Google AdMob is transmitted using Google's own encrypted connections and is subject to Google's security practices.
